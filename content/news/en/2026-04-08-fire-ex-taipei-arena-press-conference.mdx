Golden Melody-winning band Fire EX. takes on Taipei Arena for the first time in their 26-year history, inviting 10,000 "sparks" (Fire EX.'s nickname for its fans) to make July 18 a day to remember together. The band held its ticket-launch press conference today (4/8) with industry guests in attendance, with tickets going on sale this Saturday, April 11. Fire EX. couldn't hide their nerves, saying, "Playing Taipei Arena has always been our dream — it's finally happening!" Surprise guest and close friend Nine One One's Jian Zhi made the trip up specially to deliver a gift, grinning, "You'd better invite me!"

26 years into their career and previously honored with a Golden Melody Award for Best Band, Fire EX. held their ticket-launch press conference for their Taipei Arena show today (4/8), officially announcing their first-ever Taipei Arena concert this July 18, 2026. Tickets go on general sale at noon on April 11 via KKTIX. Over 26 years, Fire EX.'s music has followed countless fans from the fire of youth into building their own families. This concert marks not just a milestone in the band's journey from campus tours to arena-scale shows, but a chance to bring together 26 years of creative energy into a full-scale production, gathering 10,000 "sparks" together to create what promises to be Fire EX.'s greatest day yet, together with their fans.

![Fire EX. happily announces their Taipei Arena show (from left: bassist Pipi, guitarist Yuchen, frontman Sam, drummer Kase)](/images/news/fire-ex-taipei-arena-press-conference/fire-ex-taipei-arena-press-conference-hero.jpg "Fire EX. announces the Taipei Arena show")

All four members of Fire EX. attended today's (4/8) press conference, standing before an all-red key visual to declare their determination to hold their first-ever Taipei Arena concert. The band said: "We're deep in preparation for the Arena show right now. We've specifically brought on a music director to help us tie the transitions between songs together, and we're also discussing which guest musicians to invite. We care a lot about the meaning of each transition between songs, since those songs were written at different points in time, so all of it needs fresh arrangement to connect properly."

Well-liked across the industry, Fire EX. also invited several industry veterans to bear witness in person: Taipei Music Center chairman Liang Hsu-lun, Taipei Music Center CEO Deng Nai-tsu, Kaohsiung Music Center CEO Ting Tu-lan, and co-organizer KKLIVE chairman Huang Chen-feng and Being Studio's innovation center director Chen Chieh-li jointly announced the ticket sale date, a sign of the deep relationships Fire EX. has built across the industry over many years.

At the press conference, the band also carried out a symbolic "flag-planting ceremony," reflecting emotionally on milestones since the band formed. Fire EX. shared: "Getting Fire EX. to where it is now has been like climbing a mountain. Reaching Taipei Arena is probably like reaching the summit." During the ceremony, members reminisced about milestones including their first large-scale concert at NTU Sports Center in 2013, becoming the first band to perform at Taoyuan Baseball Stadium in 2016, and winning the Golden Melody Award for Best Band in 2020. Recalling the first time they played a 10,000-capacity show at Taoyuan Baseball Stadium, Yuchen remembered: "Back then, to build up the momentum, we ran in relay all the way from Taipei to the stadium in Taoyuan — now we just need to run from our office on Zhongxiao East Road to Taipei Arena," prompting host Jianheng to joke, "Guess you're getting older, huh," to which Sam laughed, "No relay running needed this time, we'll just run together."  Finally, Sam shared his feelings about finally reaching Taipei Arena: "We've been trying to book this landmark venue since 2020, and now we're finally about to stand on that stage — it's genuinely moving. This stage has been a dream we've held onto for a long time, and now we finally have the ability to make it happen."

![Nine One One's Jian Zhi (center) makes the trip up specially with a bouquet to congratulate the band](/images/news/fire-ex-taipei-arena-press-conference/fire-ex-taipei-arena-press-conference-guest.jpg "Jian Zhi congratulates Fire EX.")

Toward the end of the press conference, Fire EX.'s close friend, Nine One One's Jian Zhi, made a surprise appearance with a specially made fiery-red "champagne bouquet," happily saying he'd come to celebrate his friends finally "taking the arena," and that they'd be cracking open the champagne together right after. Fire EX. was thrilled to see Jian Zhi — himself a three-time Taipei Arena performer — show up to greet them, and Jian Zhi joked back, "So you'll actually invite me to come watch!" As the "senior," Jian Zhi also shared a story from his own first time at Taipei Arena: "The first time we played the Arena, we had three jet skis that needed to be lifted almost five stories up — only me and Yangcong went up there, because Chun Feng is afraid of heights, so we used an inflatable doll with his face taped on instead." He added, "Everyone was holding up blue glow sticks, and looking down at that from the stage was incredibly moving — everyone has some dream or longing tied to that venue, and the day you finally see it happen is truly moving."

"ON FIRE DAY 2026: Fire EX. Taipei Arena Concert" will take place on July 18, 2026, with tickets going on general sale via KKTIX at noon on April 11. Set your alarms — we'll see everyone racing for tickets on KKTIX this Saturday at noon. For more on the Fire EX. concert, follow the band's official website and official fan page.
