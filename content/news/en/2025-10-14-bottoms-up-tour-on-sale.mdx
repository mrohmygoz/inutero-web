Tickets are now on sale for music brand In Utero's second event series, "In Utero Present Vol. 2: Bottoms Up," bringing together L8ching, Giyu Tjuljaviya, and Erin Song — three "drinking buddies" from Beitou — for a limited-setlist tour to warm up the winter nights. From Tainan all the way back to Taipei, drinking and singing along the way, each stop pairs with a local bar for a limited-edition cocktail, building a double-layered experience.

Music brand "In Utero" launches a brand-new performance series, "In Utero Present Vol. 2: Bottoms Up," inviting L8ching, Giyu Tjuljaviya, and Erin Song to sing their way from Tainan and Taichung to Taipei across November weekend nights.

![Promotional photo of L8ching, Giyu Tjuljaviya, and Erin Song, the three "Beitou drinking buddies"](/images/news/2025-10-14-bottoms-up-tour-on-sale/bottoms-up-tour-on-sale-hero.jpg "Promotional photo for \"In Utero Present Vol. 2: Bottoms Up\"")

Following last year's "Hotpot Band Show," which brought listeners the enthusiastically received experience of "eating hotpot while listening to music" at Chan Chi Hot Pot, music brand "In Utero" is back this year with a brand-new performance series, "In Utero Present Vol. 2: Bottoms Up," inviting three musicians rooted in the mountains — L8ching, Giyu Tjuljaviya, and Erin Song — to sing their way from Tainan and Taichung to Taipei across November weekend nights, stringing together a winter night's gathering with music and drink. Tickets went on sale at noon on Tuesday, October 14, with stops at the old-house bar Chuanfa in Tainan on November 8, The Pillars in Taichung on November 15, and Witch House in Taipei on November 21.

L8ching, Giyu Tjuljaviya, and Erin Song all live in greater Taipei, and the shared instincts of musicians — plus the chemistry of two extroverts and one introvert — naturally turned them into everyday drinking buddies, often meeting up around Beitou and Shipai. One night on a Shipai street corner, drinking and talking, they decided: "Why not do a little tour?" — and that's how the "Bottoms Up" project was born. The In Utero team says they wanted to continue the curatorial spirit of "turning everyday scenes into a stage," so the music isn't just a performance but a toast between friends, a journey together. The three musicians also worked with each venue to design a signature cocktail for every stop, so the audience doesn't just hear the music but can taste each city's character too. The team adds: "Every one of these drinks is a one-night-only thing — miss it and it's gone!"

Giyu Tjuljaviya, a Paiwan singer-songwriter from Taitung, just won Best Lyricist at this year's Golden Melody Awards for "Child of the Southern Loop," singing the wind and sea of the land in a warm, sincere voice. L8ching, "the sound of a free spirit," carries the whole night with the nimble rhythm of his drummer's background, the kind of groove that makes you want to sway along, a little tipsy. Erin Song, a singer-songwriter from the Hla'alua people of Kaohsiung, moves through the scene with a delicate creative touch, her low, stylish voice like a glass of red wine in the night. The three have very different personalities, but music and friendship give them an effortless chemistry, making "In Utero Present Vol. 2: Bottoms Up" a sincere, charming winter-night gathering. The three also hint: "This setlist has a lot of surprises in store — songs we've never performed before, tour-only arrangements. Come find us for a drink!"

The In Utero team jokes: "Honestly, we just wanted an excuse to get together with friends again — this time drinking instead of eating hotpot." From last year's "eat hotpot while listening to music" Hotpot Band Show, to turning Kaitai Shengwang Temple on Jiantan Mountain into a large-scale ritual scene together with Mong Tong and Thai band JPBS, to this year's "drink and sing along the way" tour format, In Utero hopes music and art don't only happen on a stage — they can grow organically in the corners of everyday life and become part of it.

"In Utero Present Vol. 2: Bottoms Up" tickets went on sale via Ticket Plus at noon on Tuesday, October 14. Every ticket includes a signature cocktail plus a surprise drinking-themed gift, with a discounted two-person bundle at NT$1,798 ("Drinking Together" package) — bring a friend and join the "Bottoms Up" crew! For more, follow In Utero's official Facebook page, Instagram, and Threads.
