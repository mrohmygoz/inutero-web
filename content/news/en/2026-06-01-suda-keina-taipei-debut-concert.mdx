Japan's hundred-million-view, do-it-all singer-songwriter Suda Keina made his Taiwan debut on May 30, as part of the Asia tour "Suda Keina TOUR 2026 'GLIMMER.'" The Taipei Legacy stop sold out before the show, drawing over a thousand eager fans.

![Japan's hundred-million-view polymath Suda Keina makes his Taiwan debut](/images/news/2026-06-01-suda-keina-taipei-debut-concert/2026-06-01-suda-keina-taipei-debut-concert-hero.jpg "Japan's hundred-million-view polymath Suda Keina makes his Taiwan debut")

Suda Keina writes, composes, and arranges all his own work. He first broke through online in 2016 as VOCALOID producer "Balloon," whose track "Charles" has racked up over a hundred million YouTube views. He began performing under his own name in 2017, and his 2023 appearance on Japan's flagship music channel "THE FIRST TAKE" has drawn nearly 9 million views. His distinctive style and propulsive energy have won over a new generation of listeners, and he has sung theme songs for anime including Fire Force, the Assassination Classroom rerun, and Run with the Wind.

![Suda Keina writes, composes, and arranges all his own work](/images/news/2026-06-01-suda-keina-taipei-debut-concert/2026-06-01-suda-keina-taipei-debut-concert-portrait.jpg "Suda Keina writes, composes, and arranges all his own work")

**Greeting fans in Mandarin, hoping the show becomes a glimmer in everyone's life**

Suda opened with Fire Force's ending theme "veil," followed by a run of fan favorites, greeting the crowd in Mandarin along the way. The tour's theme, "GLIMMER," carries a sense of hope — Suda said life inevitably brings things we'd rather avoid, and he hopes this show can be a small glimmer in everyone's life.

He also performed "Mabel" and "Ame to Petra," originally released under the Balloon name with virtual singer v flower on vocals, calling them songs that shaped his life profoundly.

![Suda Keina fulfills a years-long promise to Taiwanese fans, finally performing here](/images/news/2026-06-01-suda-keina-taipei-debut-concert/2026-06-01-suda-keina-taipei-debut-concert-fulfilled-promise.jpg "Suda Keina fulfills a years-long promise to Taiwanese fans, finally performing here")

**A decade-old promise finally kept**

Suda shared emotionally at the close of the show that a decade ago, when he released music as Balloon, many Taiwanese fans had already been following him and leaving comments wishing for a Taiwan performance. He kept those wishes in mind — "someday I'll go to Taipei" — and worked hard in Japan hoping to repay his overseas fans. Today, he said, that promise finally came true.

Fans called for two encores including the hundred-million-view classic "Charles," moving Suda deeply. He promised to work even harder on his music back in Japan, and said he hopes to join a Taiwan music festival and return to Taipei again in the coming years.
