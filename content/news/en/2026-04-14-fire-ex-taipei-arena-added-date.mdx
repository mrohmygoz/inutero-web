Fire EX.'s first-ever Taipei Arena tickets sell out instantly 26 years into the band's career; the band posts with feeling, "something to be grateful for and proud of for a lifetime." With fans still clamoring for more, Fire EX. officially announces a second date, July 19, at Taipei Arena, with tickets going on sale at noon on April 17. "ON FIRE DAY 2026: Fire EX. Taipei Arena Concert" now runs two consecutive nights, July 18 and 19.

Golden Melody-winning band Fire EX. is set for their first-ever Taipei Arena concert on 7/18, 26 years into their career. Tickets sold out instantly when they went on sale at noon on 4/11, leaving many fans who missed out clamoring for an additional show. On the evening of 4/13, Fire EX. officially announced a second date, July 19, with tickets for the added show going on sale at noon on 4/17 via KKTIX and FamilyMart's FamiPort. News of the second date drew a flood of comments from fans: "Finally!" and "I have to get tickets this time!"

![Two-day key visual](/images/news/fire-ex-taipei-arena-added-date/fire-ex-taipei-arena-added-date-key-visual.jpg "ON FIRE DAY 2026: Fire EX. Taipei Arena Concert two-day key visual")

From the 4/1 ticket-info announcement through the 4/11 on-sale, Fire EX. posted a daily countdown video featuring a rotating cast of friends — including Lin Chih-sheng, "Taiwan's #1 Commuter Brand," Marz23, someshiit Sam, "City Founding Father" Site F4, Wu Nien-jen, Rakuten Girls, Shandong, Fear of Fat, Nine One One, and Shallow Levee frontwoman Yiling — showing off just how well-liked Fire EX. is across the industry. The band cleverly played on the phonetic similarity between friends' names and the countdown numbers of days, successfully getting fans to share and guess who'd show up in the next day's countdown, with fans posting things like "Can't wait to see who counts down tomorrow" and "Stop promoting it, what am I going to do if I can't get tickets!"

Tickets for the 7/18 Fire EX. Taipei Arena show went on sale at noon on 4/11, drawing over ten thousand simultaneous buyers online, and sold out within one minute. After the sellout, Fire EX. posted with feeling: "Thank you, truly, thank you all! Whichever song, album, or show brought you to Fire EX., we're honored to have that connection with you. Even more, it's because of everyone's support that this long-anticipated adventure started off with so much energy. Our first Taipei Arena show selling out instantly is absolutely something to be grateful for and proud of for a lifetime. We've walked 26 years to reach this milestone — thank you for walking here with us. On 7/18 and 19, let's — small as we are — create a great day together!"

![Fire EX. promotional photo](/images/news/fire-ex-taipei-arena-added-date/fire-ex-taipei-arena-added-date-portrait.jpg "Fire EX. promotional photo")

"ON FIRE DAY 2026: Fire EX. Taipei Arena Concert" takes place on July 18 and July 19, 2026. The 7/18 show is officially sold out; tickets for the added 7/19 show go on general sale via KKTIX and FamilyMart's FamiPort at noon on April 17. For more on the Fire EX. concert, follow the band's official Facebook page or Instagram.
