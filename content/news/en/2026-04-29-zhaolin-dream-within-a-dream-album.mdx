Zhaolin returns with second album "Dream Within a Dream" just one month later, co-created with his band Sweet Kiss Kiss to extend the dream into a new chapter. Lead single "A Walk" gets a music video made with director Shih Li-jen, with a physical double-album release of "Meeting in Dreams / Dream Within a Dream" promised for May. Zhaolin and Wang Chao-hua share a stage for the first time, performing collaboration track "Old Song" and a reworked tribute to Wang's classic "Ah Ba Pi Nng Kah Te."

![Zhaolin "Dream Within a Dream" digital cover](/images/news/zhaolin-dream-within-a-dream-album/zhaolin-dream-within-a-dream-album-cover.jpg "Zhaolin Dream Within a Dream digital cover")

Following the release of his debut Taiwanese-language solo album "Meeting in Dreams" in March, Taiwanese-language rising star Zhaolin returns this month (April) with a new album co-created with his band Sweet Kiss Kiss, "Dream Within a Dream" — an extension of the dream into a side chapter, delivering nineteen songs and showing off an extraordinary creative output. "Dream Within a Dream" is built around a full band arrangement, keeping the electronic-music foundation while injecting more physical, rock-driven energy for a sound that feels closer to a live show. After the album dropped, "Zhaolin & Sweet Kiss Kiss" were invited to perform at the mid-April Du Shu Festival, sharing a stage for the first time with Wang Chao-hua, who contributed to the album — performing their collaboration track "Old Song" alongside a surprising reworked version of Wang's classic "Ah Ba Pi Nng Kah Te" that won over plenty of fans in the crowd.

On the making of "Dream Within a Dream," Zhaolin says: "Honestly, it really has to do with working closely with Sweet Kiss Kiss on live shows during the making of 'Meeting in Dreams.' That period got me thinking about what it would be like to go into a studio and record some tracks together with the band. I happened to still have some demos that didn't make it onto 'Meeting in Dreams,' plus more ideas I still wanted to write, so I decided to make something with the band that felt like a side story, but also extended into its own chapter."

Comparing the two albums' approach, Zhaolin describes "Meeting in Dreams" as built mainly around electronic-music concepts — strong repetition and collage, with a softer, hazier sound treatment that feels like floating, surrounded from the center. "Dream Within a Dream" brings in more tactile feel and weight, with the lyrics and melody developing alongside the instrumentation and chord progressions, making the verse-chorus structure and dynamic swings more pronounced — in post-production, he wanted it to feel more like a focused live show, with every sound sliding clearly past the ear — two distinctly different works that still echo one another.

![Zhaolin and Wang Chao-hua perform together at the 2026 Du Shu Festival](/images/news/zhaolin-dream-within-a-dream-album/zhaolin-dream-within-a-dream-album-duet.jpg "Zhaolin and Wang Chao-hua perform together")

The album's Dolby Atmos mix was handled by producer Adrian Chan, who shares: "'Dream Within a Dream' has layers of guitar, synthesizer, and vocals stacked throughout the arrangements — it's extremely cinematic, which made it a great fit for spatial audio mixing."

Zhaolin's collaboration with the band "Sweet Kiss Kiss" traces back to a first performance invitation two years ago: "Back then I'd just released two singles and honestly had no real concept of forming a band or doing live shows. It was only after that show that I started seriously thinking about what 'live' meant, and slowly began wanting to extend the album's sound into a band format. I later brought in KX, who plays modular synths, and Lin Zhuan, who was the vocal engineer on both of my albums, and we started exploring how a studio recording could become a live performance. The current lineup is electric guitarist BB, Lin Zhuan, synths and keys from KX, and bass and acoustic guitar from Yi Yun. The whole band really grew step by step through performing and collaborating together."

Zhaolin also released the music video for "Dream Within a Dream"'s lead single "A Walk" yesterday (the 28th), made with director Shih Li-jen. Zhaolin shares: "The director's response to this song came through mostly in the imagery, rather than a lot of verbal analysis. To me, that felt like a kind of secret understanding — each of us brought our own reading to it, and what came out in the end made me really happy. I love that way of working together." Both "Meeting in Dreams" and "Dream Within a Dream" were produced in Dolby Atmos by Adrian Chan, who has worked with Anthony Wong, Denise Ho, and Sandee Chan, and who shares: "'Dream Within a Dream' has layers of guitar, synthesizer, and vocals stacked throughout the arrangements — it's extremely cinematic, which made it a great fit for spatial audio mixing," hoping to give listeners a richer sensory experience.

In mid-April, Zhaolin & Sweet Kiss Kiss were also invited to perform at the Du Shu Festival, where, beyond playing songs from the new album, they shared a stage with Wang Chao-hua for the first time — performing not only "Old Song," their collaboration track from "Meeting in Dreams," but also a reworked tribute to Wang's classic "Ah Ba Pi Nng Kah Te." Zhaolin says: "This was really our first time performing together formally. She gave me a lot of freedom throughout the collaboration — whether it was covering 'Ah Ba Pi Nng Kah Te' or performing 'Old Song,' which we co-wrote together, both were actually things I proposed, and I was so happy she was willing to perform that song publicly again after such a long time." Zhaolin also reveals that while preparing the cover, they kept thinking about how to stay respectful while still putting their own feeling into it: "During the process, we realized it actually suited a dub texture really well, so we developed it in that direction. In the end, Sister Chao-hua told me she really loved this version, which made me really happy too."

Zhaolin's two-chapter album "Meeting in Dreams / Dream Within a Dream" is expected to get a physical release in May — stay tuned. For more on Zhaolin, follow his Facebook page and Instagram.
