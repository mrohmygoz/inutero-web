The autumn music event "VOICES 2026" arrives at Taipei's Dajia Riverside Park on October 3–4! Organizers revealed the festival's first major lineup wave today (8/4): the cool, soulful Leah Dou, triple-Golden-award vocalist Crowd Lu, rock powerhouse TRASH, Golden Melody duo Crispy, plus new-generation voices puppiebelly, Hsu Han-kuang Lumi, Guandu Teens, and JOYCE. The two-day event brings together 40 acts across four stages, with top Mandopop creators from across cities converging on Taipei.

![VOICES 2026, First Wave Lineup](/images/news/2026-08-04-voices-2026-lineup-wave-1/2026-08-04-voices-2026-lineup-wave-1-hero.jpg "VOICES 2026, First Wave Lineup")

Curated by three close friends — veteran producer Chen Chien-chi, musician Han Li-kang, and Underworld Music founder Oliver — "VOICES" has drawn attention since it was announced, with the limited "blind-bird" two-day tickets selling out within days of release. Organizers say "VOICES" represents sounds that refuse to be boxed in, inviting fans to gather riverside in crisp October air and feel the resonance of music together.

![Leah Dou's distinctive ethereal voice and freewheeling, avant-garde music have won fans worldwide](/images/news/2026-08-04-voices-2026-lineup-wave-1/2026-08-04-voices-2026-lineup-wave-1-leah-dou.jpg "Leah Dou's distinctive ethereal voice and freewheeling, avant-garde music have won fans worldwide")

This first wave is packed with highlights. Leah Dou, rarely seen at Taiwan festivals in recent years, brings her singular ethereal voice and freewheeling, experimental sound for a soulful listening experience. Crowd Lu, the triple-Golden-award vocalist whose tours sell out show after show, arrives with explosive energy and a warm, piercing voice to soothe the whole crowd. Rising psychedelic voice puppiebelly makes her Taiwan debut — once named an Apple Music "Rising Star" — with her late-2025 album "Girl, Fly" drawing wider buzz.

![Triple-Golden-award vocalist Crowd Lu brings a warm voice that heals the crowd](/images/news/2026-08-04-voices-2026-lineup-wave-1/2026-08-04-voices-2026-lineup-wave-1-crowd-lu.jpg "Triple-Golden-award vocalist Crowd Lu brings a warm voice that heals the crowd")

Beyond the three headliners, this wave gathers more of Taiwan's top musical talent: high-energy Golden Melody rock band TRASH bringing the most electrifying mosh of the night; Golden Melody Best Vocal Duo Crispy, soothing urban restlessness with tender, poetic harmonies. Poet-musician Hsu Han-kuang Lumi, youth-beloved band Guandu Teens, and Gen-Z favorite JOYCE also share the bill, showing off the rawest, most diverse "voices" in contemporary Mandopop.

VOICES' early-bird two-day tickets went on sale August 4 — limited quantities, while supplies last, so music fans are encouraged to grab tickets fast.
