Elephant Gym releases new single "Highway," their first new work since the band went on an 18-month hiatus in 2025. The song charts a mindset of starting over through the image of a road trip, delivered in pure instrumental form — a return to the band's most essential mode of songwriting. The single's music video is set to premiere 6/25.

![Golden Melody band Elephant Gym (illustrative photo)](/images/news/2026-06-23-elephant-gym-highway-single/2026-06-23-elephant-gym-highway-single-hero.jpg "Golden Melody band Elephant Gym (illustrative photo)")

"Highway" began as a tapping bassline bassist Kai-Ting wrote during the hiatus, then grew with guitarist Kai-Hsiang's melody and arrangement, before drummer Chia-Chin built out the rhythm — ultimately becoming a piece about movement, journeys, and the passage of time. The song opens with a bright guitar solo, like the wide view that opens up once a car hits the highway; as drums and bass join in, the music keeps pushing forward. Bassist Kai-Ting shared: "The moment I wrote that tapping line, it already felt like it kept pushing forward — a lot like driving on a highway."

For Elephant Gym, "the road" isn't just a creative metaphor. The band has toured across Asia, Europe, and the Americas for years, and long stretches on the road have become part of daily life. True to the song's imagery, the "Highway" music video was shot across three countries: band members drove from Kaohsiung northward themselves, documenting Taiwan's roads along the way, while US-based director Dong-Ren Hong filmed American road footage, and the band's Japanese management team captured Japanese streets and roads.

Toward the end of the video, the footage shifts from road scenery back to the members' daily lives — unscripted moments that became the part of the video closest to the band's real life. For a band that has toured for years, moving has long been the norm, and every journey carries both leaving and coming home.

Elephant Gym is currently preparing for their comeback "Instrumental / Vocal" double tour, with four shows — 7/24 and 7/25 in Taipei, 8/31 and 8/14 in Kaohsiung — featuring Bear Hug Band and I'm A Motor Girl as opening guests for the instrumental and vocal nights respectively. Beyond Taiwan, the band will also play a show at Tokyo's WWWX in September, with all five comeback shows selling out within the week tickets went on sale. The band has also been invited to play Japanese independent label Flake Records' 20th anniversary festival in September — the only Taiwanese band on the bill.
