Japanese Gen-Z artist kiyu rides a wave of buzz onto the Chisheng Zaodong Festival stage, bringing new single "Circus" to meet Taiwanese fans. Co-written with Galileo Galilei guitarist Fumito Iwai, the track renders a Gen-Z view of "near-future" anxiety and restlessness — a performance fans are bracing for.

![kiyu's "kiyu FIRST LIVE IN TAIPEI 2026" sold out at THE WALL this February](/images/news/2026-05-26-kiyu-circus-chisheng-zaodong-festival/2026-05-26-kiyu-circus-chisheng-zaodong-festival-hero.jpg "kiyu's \"kiyu FIRST LIVE IN TAIPEI 2026\" sold out at THE WALL this February")

**A debut that peaked instantly — first single topped 730,000 views**

kiyu began uploading tracks to VOCALOID in 2024, breaking through as a Gen-Z voice while still a university student. Her songwriting cuts straight through adolescent melancholy with a clear, piercing voice, confronting the vulnerability people usually keep hidden. Her first official digital single, "Kizuato," has topped 730,000 views on YouTube.

![kiyu held her first overseas solo concert, "kiyu FIRST LIVE IN TAIPEI 2026," at THE WALL in Taipei this February](/images/news/2026-05-26-kiyu-circus-chisheng-zaodong-festival/2026-05-26-kiyu-circus-chisheng-zaodong-festival-live-in-taipei.jpg "kiyu held her first overseas solo concert, \"kiyu FIRST LIVE IN TAIPEI 2026,\" at THE WALL in Taipei this February")

**Debut Taipei show sold out early in the year; new single "Circus" leans into the near-future**

kiyu's first overseas solo concert, "kiyu FIRST LIVE IN TAIPEI 2026," sold out at THE WALL this February before any major media coverage. Her quality has also earned recognition in Japan's industry — early work was mixed personally by Galileo Galilei guitarist Fumito Iwai.

This March, kiyu released her fifth digital single, "Circus," with Iwai as lead arranger. The two refined the track together in a private Sapporo studio, rendering "near-future" anxiety and restlessness through a distinctive City Pop sensibility.

![kiyu's debut overseas concert sold out immediately on ticket release, drawing strong attention from Taiwanese fans](/images/news/2026-05-26-kiyu-circus-chisheng-zaodong-festival/2026-05-26-kiyu-circus-chisheng-zaodong-festival-sold-out.jpg "kiyu's debut overseas concert sold out immediately on ticket release, drawing strong attention from Taiwanese fans")

**First overseas festival appearance — light electronic aesthetics sweep into Taichung**

Following her sold-out debut show earlier this year, kiyu answers Taiwanese fans' call once more, taking the Chisheng Zaodong Festival stage on May 30. She says she's looking forward to sharing a bill with Taiwan's independent bands, and has even been asking fans online about Taichung weather and outfit suggestions. Fans of contemporary J-pop and City Pop won't want to miss this.
