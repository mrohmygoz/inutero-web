Golden Melody Best Band Fire EX. closed out the second night of "2026 ON FIRE DAY Taipei Arena Concert" today (19th), drawing another 10,000-plus crowd to witness the band's biggest milestone yet. Frontman Yang Dazheng declared the band has no plans to slow down, revealing a trip to the US next month to record a new album, and surprised the crowd further by announcing a Kaohsiung Arena date on December 12 — a third arena show within the year. With 11,000 fans backing them tonight, Fire EX. opened the largest mosh pit Taipei Arena has ever seen; fans refused to let the night end, calling for a second encore that stretched the set to 33 songs.

![Fire EX. marks 26 years with their first arena run — two nights sold out 20,000 tickets, with the band thanking fans emotionally: "Thank you for bringing us here"](/images/news/2026-07-19-fire-ex-taipei-arena-night-two-recap/2026-07-19-fire-ex-taipei-arena-night-two-recap-hero.jpg "Fire EX. marks 26 years with their first arena run — two nights sold out 20,000 tickets, with the band thanking fans emotionally: \"Thank you for bringing us here\"")

Frontman Dazheng choked up more than once tonight, thanking the family members in attendance during "Words from the Heart." After the duet "For My Daughter" with guitarist Yu-Chen, Yu-Chen shared what it's been like becoming a new father, filling the arena with warmth.

This Arena run also carried the band's biggest production budget to date, spending eight figures on a first-of-its-kind flying stage, performing hits like "Sailing to a Distant Place" and "People of the Sea" from mid-air as the whole crowd sang along in a wave of emotion.

![A flying stage, built at great expense, becomes "People of the Sea"](/images/news/2026-07-19-fire-ex-taipei-arena-night-two-recap/2026-07-19-fire-ex-taipei-arena-night-two-recap-flying-stage.jpg "A flying stage, built at great expense, becomes \"People of the Sea\"")

Dazheng said sincerely: "It turns out our music really does resonate and give people strength — that's the greatest validation we could ask for as musicians. Thank you for bringing us here, and for telling us Fire EX. really does deserve two Arena nights. Don't worry." Longtime album producer Mike Green also made a special appearance in support tonight.

Yang Dazheng shared that he always chokes up singing "Taiwan 11" in rehearsal, recalling how founding Fire Music a decade ago came during the band's lowest point. As the crowd roared for more, fans ultimately won a second encore, with the band adding "Drinking Today," "Back to Woha," and "Your House Exploded, I Didn't Cry" for a total of 33 songs — leaving night-two fans calling it an absolute steal.

As the show closed, Fire EX. dropped one more surprise: Yang Dazheng announced a Kaohsiung Arena date for "2026 ON FIRE DAY" on December 12, inviting all fans to reunite before year's end.

![Fire EX. group portrait, left to right: Ko Guang, Yu-Chen, Dazheng, Pi-Pi](/images/news/2026-07-19-fire-ex-taipei-arena-night-two-recap/2026-07-19-fire-ex-taipei-arena-night-two-recap-group-portrait.jpg "Fire EX. group portrait, left to right: Ko Guang, Yu-Chen, Dazheng, Pi-Pi")

"ON FIRE DAY 2026 Fire EX. Kaohsiung Arena Concert" takes place December 12 at Kaohsiung Arena, with tickets going on sale via KKTIX and Famiport at noon on Friday, 7/31. For more, visit the band's official website or Facebook page.
