aDAN's "Lonely Goldfish: A Practice in Freedom" hits Nangang SUB on May 3, with hip-hop artist Gummy B joining her on stage. "A lot of things take practice — so does freedom," aDAN says: real freedom isn't about running away, it's about coming back to yourself. The setlist moves from dusk through night into dawn, with brand-new unreleased songs premiering live for the first time. A wish planted at the 2023 Golden Indie Music Awards finally comes true as aDAN brings Gummy B on stage.

Urban singer-songwriter aDAN will hold her new solo show "Lonely Goldfish: A Practice in Freedom" at Nangang SUB LIVE in Taipei on Sunday, May 3. Following her 2025 album *Field Guide to a Lonely Goldfish* and its mini-tour, the show gathers her personal musical energy and a deeper self-exploration, examining the two faces of solitude and freedom. aDAN also surprised fans yesterday with the announcement that hip-hop artist Gummy B will join the show, bringing a live-only surprise performance for a journey dedicated to herself — and one she's inviting the audience to step into with her.

!["Lonely Goldfish: A Practice in Freedom" key visual](/images/news/2026-04-16-adan-lonely-goldfish-concert/adan-lonely-goldfish-concert-key-visual.jpg "Lonely Goldfish: A Practice in Freedom key visual")

*Field Guide to a Lonely Goldfish* uses the goldfish as a metaphor, capturing the many faces and feelings of modern city life. The subtitle of this show, "A Practice in Freedom," reflects a deeper realization aDAN arrived at after the album's release. "Honestly, I hesitated over this subtitle at first — 'freedom' is such a huge subject." But after turning it over again and again, she became certain that a lot of things take practice, and freedom is one of them. "I used to think freedom meant running away, going somewhere more open. But real freedom isn't reaching outward — it's moving closer to yourself." Letting go of others' expectations, letting go of being hard on yourself, slowly loosening what you've been gripping tightly — that's how you come back to yourself and become yourself. She has a message for fans: "Let's come back to ourselves and become ourselves together, like practicing how to love someone all over again."

aDAN also gave a preview of the setlist's arc — to avoid spoiling the surprise, she describes it as a journey that "starts in golden dusk, crosses through the night, and arrives at dawn." The songs move from imagining freedom, gradually asking "what exactly is holding me back," through to accepting that solitude and freedom can coexist, arriving at the freedom she understands now. Along with pulling together songs from her debut EP *Too Quiet*, her album *Flashback*, and *Field Guide to a Lonely Goldfish*, she also revealed she'll perform brand-new, never-released songs: "It's going to be musically rich — I'm ready to really let loose!"

![aDAN](/images/news/2026-04-16-adan-lonely-goldfish-concert/adan-lonely-goldfish-concert-portrait.jpg "aDAN")

The live band draws on aDAN's deep musical network built up over years — Pink Noise's Chen Wei-ta serves as band leader and guitarist, joined by keyboardist Guo Jun-lin (Marswalk), bassist Wang Yu-chia (Geometry Code), drummer Wu Cheng-di, and programmer Tsai Yi-ju: "Almost all the musicians this time make their own music too, so there's a ton of creative energy — the live show is guaranteed to turn heads and sound great." aDAN also surprised fans yesterday on social media by announcing Gummy B as a special guest, sharing that she watched Gummy B perform up close at the 2023 Golden Indie Music Awards and was so moved she nearly cried — quietly planting a wish to collaborate with him someday. Later, while working on *Field Guide to a Lonely Goldfish*, she wrote a demo that instinctively felt perfect for him, and with guitarist Chen Wei-ta being a mutual collaborator of both artists, several threads of fate came together to make that wish happen unexpectedly.

To promote the show, aDAN has pulled out all the stops — not only shooting a brand-new key visual, but personally editing every promotional video herself, earning comments like "her mental state is as beautiful as she is." She admits she expected it to be a struggle but ended up unexpectedly enjoying it, and plans to finish it all with a sense of fun. This week, aDAN even posted a video introducing the show in fluent Japanese on her personal account, showing off a hidden skill that drew praise: "She'd make a great Japanese actress!" On top of that, a sudden gust of wind hit Taipei during the key-visual photoshoot, leaving the whole crew disheveled with runny noses and windswept hair as they pushed through the shoot. aDAN laughed, "Everybody's hair turned into a madwoman's — it was rough," but the effort paid off, and fans flooded the comments with praise once the photos were released: "The key visual is gorgeous! Then again, she always is."

Looking back on the mini-tour and various shows for *Field Guide to a Lonely Goldfish*, aDAN shares that at the Tainan stop, she choked up mid-performance of "What Kind of Adult" and couldn't keep singing for the first time — "the moment the fans picked up the song and carried it for me, I felt like I'd been caught." She thanked the fans who've stayed with her all along, saying, "Getting to wander freely through music makes me so happy!" She hopes the audience comes into this show with a relaxed heart, ready to practice freedom together, and that everyone leaves with something deeply personal.
