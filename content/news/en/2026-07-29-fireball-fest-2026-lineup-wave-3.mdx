"FIREBALL Fest." curated by Golden Melody band Fire EX. returns to Lotte Taoyuan Baseball Stadium this 11/14–11/15. After unveiling its first wave (Fire EX., Dragon Ash) and second wave (Sambomaster, Crystal Lake, Isyana Sarasvati), the festival reveals its third lineup wave today: Shoalin, EGO-WRAPPIN', Ureuk and The Gypsies, Hsien Ching, Gummy B, Random, PiA Wu Pei-ya, EmptyORio, and debloop — a lineup spanning Taiwan, Japan, and Korea across hip-hop, jazz, rock, and punk.

![FIREBALL Fest. 2026 reveals third lineup wave (illustrative photo)](/images/news/2026-07-29-fireball-fest-2026-lineup-wave-3/2026-07-29-fireball-fest-2026-lineup-wave-3-hero.jpg "FIREBALL Fest. 2026 reveals third lineup wave (illustrative photo)")

Formed in Osaka in 1996, EGO-WRAPPIN' — vocalist Yoshie Nakano and guitarist Masaki Mori — blends jazz, Showa-era pop, and blues into every release, earning a reputation from Japanese media as one of the bands that best represents contemporary Japanese spirit. Known for theme songs to the dramas Okawabata Tanteisha and Only You Want to Live in Kichijoji?, EGO-WRAPPIN' marks their 30th anniversary in 2026 with a new album and a return to Taiwan's stage.

Making their FIREBALL Fest. debut this year, Gummy B and Hsien Ching are among the most closely watched new voices in Taiwan's hip-hop scene. Hsien Ching broke through with "Qing Qing" and recently won Golden Melody Best New Artist; Gummy B became a breakout star on The Big Hip Hop Era Season 2, finishing as runner-up and taking home Best Live Performance and Best Hip-Hop Song at the 14th Golden Indie Music Awards.

South Korea's Ureuk and The Gypsies, an avant-garde act fusing traditional Korean music with jazz, earned glowing reviews from Korean media with their 2025 debut album, winning Best Rookie at Korea's Pentaport Music Festival and opening for British band Shame's Korean show. FIREBALL Fest. marks their first-ever Taiwan performance.

The Taiwanese lineup shines just as bright: beyond Gummy B and Hsien Ching, there's cyber-Taike band Shoalin, fresh off a sold-out arena show and making their fifth FIREBALL Fest. appearance; PiA Wu Pei-ya, newly crowned Golden Melody Best Taiwanese Female Vocalist; EmptyORio, bringing their latest album ALL THE BEST; Taiwanese-language punk band Random; and rock band debloop.

With three waves now revealed, the 2026 FIREBALL Fest. lineup is nearly complete, with Fire EX. leading a heavyweight international bill including Dragon Ash, Sambomaster, EGO-WRAPPIN', Crystal Lake, and Isyana Sarasvati — proof of the festival's continued push to broaden its sound and its international curatorial vision.
