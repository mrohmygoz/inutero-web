Hakka singer-songwriter Han Huang, recently recognized at the Golden Melody Awards for her latest album "Ngai," kicks off her mini-tour "Finding Ngai: City Exchange Diary" at the end of this month (August), inviting four "host" guests to join her: Suming, L8ching, Zhang Wu, and Wing of Hong Kong's COPAK.

![Han Huang, official promotional photo](/images/news/2026-08-11-han-huang-tour-announcement/2026-08-11-han-huang-tour-announcement-hero.jpg "Han Huang, official promotional photo")

"Ngai" means "I" in Hakka, faithfully tracing Han's meticulous self-examination from darkness toward light. Extending the "Ngai" album's concept, which blends Hakka with multiple languages and collaborating vocalists across diverse styles, this tour is set in each collaborator's hometown, with that artist joining as a guest at each stop: L8ching in Taipei on 8/28, rapper Zhang Wu in Taichung on 9/18, Suming in Taitung on 9/20, and COPAK's Wing in Hong Kong on 10/3. Each stop will feature the first-ever live performance of their respective collaborative track, alongside a full run through the new album.

![Han Huang, "Finding Ngai Map" mini-tour key visual](/images/news/2026-08-11-han-huang-tour-announcement/2026-08-11-han-huang-tour-announcement-tour-key-visual.jpg "Han Huang, \"Finding Ngai Map\" mini-tour key visual")

The "Finding Ngai Map" mini-tour will be performed unplugged, up close with the audience. The first stop, later this month, lands at Blackbird Coffee in Beitou, hometown of singer L8ching, who sings a French verse on their collaboration "Late Spring." The second stop lands in Taichung, hometown of rapper Zhang Wu, where the two trade verses in Hakka and Taiwanese on their collaboration "Why."

The third stop in Taitung features Amis Golden Melody singer Suming — both artists recently recognized at this year's Golden Melody Awards, and both have previously covered each other's songs. The final stop crosses the strait to Hong Kong, home turf of COPAK's Wing — Han's first-ever Hong Kong performance — where she's especially looking forward to Wing's live saxophone on their collaboration "No Need for Sorrow."

![Han Huang wins Best Hakka Album at the 37th Golden Melody Awards](/images/news/2026-08-11-han-huang-tour-announcement/2026-08-11-han-huang-tour-announcement-golden-melody-award.jpg "Han Huang wins Best Hakka Album at the 37th Golden Melody Awards")

"No Need for Sorrow," her collaboration with Wing, weaves Hakka and Cantonese together, describing how Han and Wing — living separately in Taipei and Hong Kong — carry the same burdens, a letter of comfort to their generation and a love letter to their dreams.

The "Finding Ngai Map: City Exchange Diary" mini-tour is Han's first tour since her Golden Melody win. She says: "I'm so happy the work was recognized, and I hope to show even more sides of myself through future work and performances. Thank you to my family, friends, and team for their support — it's been almost two months since the win and we're still celebrating in every way possible. Come join us at the mini-tour and keep the celebration going!"

Tickets for Han Huang's "Finding Ngai Map: City Exchange Diary" mini-tour are on sale now — for more, check Han Huang's official social channels and the KKTIX ticketing platform.
