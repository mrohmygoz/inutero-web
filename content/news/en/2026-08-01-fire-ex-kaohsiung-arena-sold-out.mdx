Golden Melody band Fire EX. just wrapped their two-night Taipei Arena run on 7/18–7/19 to glowing reviews. At the show, the band announced a triumphant homecoming to Kaohsiung Arena, sending the crowd into a frenzy. "ON FIRE DAY 2026 Fire EX. Kaohsiung Arena Concert" takes the stage December 12 — the band's first-ever Kaohsiung Arena show in their 26-year history, following their Taipei Arena debut.

![Fire EX.'s 12/12 Kaohsiung Arena show sells out instantly, prompting an emergency 12/13 date (illustrative photo)](/images/news/2026-08-01-fire-ex-kaohsiung-arena-sold-out/2026-08-01-fire-ex-kaohsiung-arena-sold-out-hero.jpg "Fire EX.'s 12/12 Kaohsiung Arena show sells out instantly, prompting an emergency 12/13 date (illustrative photo)")

The Kaohsiung show's countdown video again drew a roster of celebrity friends, including local Kaohsiung food vendors, Kaohsiung influencer DiDi, YouTuber ZHI7, actress Sun Shu-mei (who once played Dazheng's on-screen wife), ELLEGARDEN frontman Hosomi Takeshi, badminton star Tai Tzu-ying, CTBC Steelers head coach Hung Yi-chung, and Sheng Xiang & Band frontman Lin Sheng-xiang. The moment tickets went on sale (7/31, 12:00), fans flooded in and sold out the show within minutes — Fire EX. responded with an emergency second date on 12/13, officially unlocking a "two arenas, two added dates" achievement.

Talking about the Kaohsiung show, the band couldn't hide their excitement: "After the Arena shows, we thought that was it — time to prep the next album. We never expected everyone's enthusiasm would bring us home to break another arena, with two shows no less!" The band says the Kaohsiung production will carry the same all-out scale as the Taipei shows, giving Kaohsiung fans a night just as spectacular.

On the added date, Dazheng specially thanked the fans who've stuck with the band, posting: "Thank you all for your support — I'm almost too moved to speak. Playing Kaohsiung Arena, right next to our alma mater [Sanmin Commercial High School], means something really special. Just thank you, thank you, thank you." The post drew a flood of comments like "welcome home to your home turf!" and "Kaohsiung, stand strong!" — showing just how much support southern Taiwan fans have for the band.

"ON FIRE DAY 2026 Fire EX. Kaohsiung Arena Concert" runs two nights, December 12 and 13, 2026. The 12/12 show has officially sold out; tickets for the added 12/13 date go on sale at noon on 8/8. For more, follow Fire EX. on Facebook or Instagram (@fireex_official).
