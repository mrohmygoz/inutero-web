First headline wave: Music Awards Japan's Best DJ winner DJ NOBU, Italian newcomer Alarico, and a global lineup converge — early-bird tickets sold out almost immediately. Now in its 13th edition, the festival keeps its ambition connected to Seoul, Tokyo, and Hong Kong's underground scenes.

This April 24–26, the 13th Organik Festival returns to the same coastal peninsula setting it has long called home — hailed as one of the best festivals on earth and a standout party in Asia. The first announced headliners include Alarico, Andy Martin, Diskonnected, DJ NOBU, Livwutang, and 15 more artists from home and abroad, spanning day into night to connect different generations and scenes of sound — dancing from dusk until dawn, an escape from the everyday.

![Organik Festival takes participants from dusk into dawn](/images/news/organik-festival-2026-early-bird-sold-out/organik-festival-2026-early-bird-sold-out-hero.jpg "Organik Festival takes participants from dusk into dawn")

The first wave includes Alarico's dense, feral 90s techno rhythms with a bad, alluring pulse; Andy Martin's Indigenous-futurist cosmology and his fast, driving Latin textures; and Diskonnected — a core member and creative force behind Organik Festival — whose ability to move between styles has helped shape Taiwan's avant-garde electronic scene.

DJ NOBU, who won Music Awards Japan's Best DJ prize last year, brings the depth built from Chiba's underground scene to a world stage, with unpredictable command of the dancefloor and an eclectic, shapeshifting selector's vocabulary. Livwutang, a fixture of North America's outsider and cult club and festival circuit, brings her own warm, exuberant atmosphere.

![DJ NOBU](/images/news/organik-festival-2026-early-bird-sold-out/organik-festival-2026-early-bird-sold-out-dj-nobu.jpg "DJ NOBU")

Rounding out the lineup are representative DJs from neighboring Asian cities and local selectors ranging from veterans to rising names. This year's fine-tuned Organik Stage still commands its mountain-and-sea vista, linking the Red Pillars ruins space and the ambient Golden Arc greenhouse, alongside food stalls and wellness workshops.

Ahead of the festival itself, Organik Takeover has been touring across continents — Wonderfruit late last year, followed by Bangkok, Tokyo this year, with Singapore and Korea's Permit still to come, and a Pacific crossing to New York and Detroit in May. Early-bird tickets sold out last week; three-day passes and four-person packages are now on sale. For the full lineup, side events, and directions, follow Organik Festival on Facebook and Instagram.
