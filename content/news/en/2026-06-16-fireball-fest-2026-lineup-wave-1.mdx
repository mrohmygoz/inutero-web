"FIREBALL Fest." curated by Golden Melody-winning band Fire EX. returns to Lotte Taoyuan Baseball Stadium this 11/14–11/15. Today (6/16) the festival revealed its first lineup wave: beyond curator Fire EX., fresh off storming Taipei Arena earlier this year, the festival has landed legendary band Dragon Ash, making a comeback after 8 years at fans' long-standing request. Early-bird two-day tickets go on sale 6/24 at noon, with all ticket types opening the following day, 6/25 at noon, via Tixcraft.

![FIREBALL Fest. 2026 reveals first lineup wave, led by Fire EX. with Dragon Ash returning after 8 years (illustrative photo)](/images/news/2026-06-16-fireball-fest-2026-lineup-wave-1/2026-06-16-fireball-fest-2026-lineup-wave-1-hero.jpg "FIREBALL Fest. 2026 reveals first lineup wave, led by Fire EX. with Dragon Ash returning after 8 years (illustrative photo)")

Dragon Ash has racked up countless million-selling records, topping Japan's Oricon chart with their third album, Viva La Revolution. Tracks like "Grateful Days" and "Life Goes On" remain rock classics to this day. Beyond their recordings, Dragon Ash is widely regarded across Japan as a band "born for live performance," known for an unmatched ability to electrify a crowd — a perennial headliner at major international festivals.

Fire EX. shared a bill with Dragon Ash back in 2018 and were captivated by their commanding live presence, hoping to one day invite the legends to an event of their own. That wish comes true at FIREBALL Fest. 2026.

Beyond the dream lineup, the festival continues to benefit from its site at Lotte Taoyuan Baseball Stadium, praised for comfortable facilities, one-stop food and entertainment, and family-friendly grounds. Last year's festival introduced two-day reserved seating for the first time; to meet growing demand, organizers will expand the seating section this year so every fan can find their ideal vantage point.

Now in its sixth edition since launching in 2017, FIREBALL Fest. has maintained a consistently high curatorial standard and distinctive musical taste, having hosted heavyweight acts like Japanese punk band WANIMA, legendary band ELLEGARDEN, Korean rap star Lee Young-ji, and Japan's Tokyo Ska Paradise Orchestra — earning its reputation as "a festival to experience once in a lifetime."
