"FIREBALL Fest." curated by Golden Melody band Fire EX. returns to Lotte Taoyuan Baseball Stadium this 11/14–11/15. After unveiling its first wave — Fire EX. and legendary Japanese rock band Dragon Ash — the festival reveals its second lineup wave today (7/7), spanning Japan, Indonesia, and Taiwan: led by Japanese rock giant Sambomaster, known for the Naruto opening theme and the Densha Otoko theme song, alongside Japanese metal band Crystal Lake, Indonesian singer-songwriter Isyana Sarasvati, and Taiwanese Golden Melody acts Flesh Juicer and Enno Cheng, plus Flesh Juicer, 2nd Base Captain, and BUTTER RIOT fresh off new releases.

![Golden Melody band Flesh Juicer](/images/news/2026-07-07-fireball-fest-2026-lineup-wave-2/2026-07-07-fireball-fest-2026-lineup-wave-2-hero.jpg "Golden Melody band Flesh Juicer")

Sambomaster formed in 2000, with frontman/guitarist Takashi Yamaguchi, bassist Yoichi Kondo, and drummer Taishi Kiuchi. Yamaguchi's candid lyrics, fused with soul- and funk-inflected punk rock plus magnetic stage presence, have won the band diehard fans across generations. Their 2004 single "Seishun Kyousoukyoku" became the Naruto opening theme, and their performance of the Densha Otoko theme the following year sent their popularity soaring. Making their first Taiwan appearance in 8 years, Sambomaster brings a knockout blast of rock energy to stir up everyone's summer memories of youth.

Also joining this wave is Japanese metal powerhouse Crystal Lake, whose sound fuses hardcore and metalcore into addictive melodic lines over dense, heavy walls of sound. With their 2026 album The Weight of Sound, the band has embarked on a world tour.

Notably, Crystal Lake and Flesh Juicer share a close bond, having performed together multiple times — from a joint show in Taipei in 2023 to the joint "HYPERSPACE JAPAN TOUR" in 2026. Flesh Juicer, playing every day of this year's FIREBALL Fest., has also previewed a new album coming this year and teased a special performance unlike anything they've done before.

The festival has also partnered internationally this year with Indonesia's LaLaLa Festival — Indonesian national treasure Isyana Sarasvati will take the FIREBALL Fest. stage, while Taiwanese representative Wendy Wander will play LaLaLa Festival in Indonesia this August through the same cross-festival connection. Isyana Sarasvati, Indonesia's most iconic singer-songwriter with over 10 million Instagram followers, blends classical, pop, and progressive rock in her music.

Beyond the international heavyweights, the Taiwanese lineup is equally strong: Golden Melody Best Band winner Flesh Juicer, Golden Melody Best Taiwanese Female Vocalist Enno Cheng, Shoalin fresh off their album The Silent Masterpiece, metal-rock fusion act Gugudan, and rising acts 2nd Base Captain and BUTTER RIOT.

FIREBALL Fest. has already sold out early-bird tickets, with presale tickets counting down — once presale sells out, no door tickets will be released, so fans are urged to act fast.
