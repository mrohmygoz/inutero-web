Elephant Gym guitarist Kai-Hsiang Chang teams up with 2026 Chiayi Rock Fest to launch the "Chiayi New Voice Audition," cultivating Taiwan's next generation of musicians, with judges including Tribe of Noise organizer Hsiao Ta-chien and Elephant Gym themselves — winners will share a stage with Fire EX. and Shoalin.

![Elephant Gym guitarist Kai-Hsiang Chang](/images/news/2026-08-14-elephant-gym-chiayi-new-voice-audition/2026-08-14-elephant-gym-chiayi-new-voice-audition-hero.jpg "Elephant Gym guitarist Kai-Hsiang Chang")

Kai-Hsiang Chang, guitarist for Golden Melody band Elephant Gym, founded his music-education brand "Micro Movement" in 2010, running songwriting camps, talks, and workshops in his hometown of Kaohsiung for years, guiding over a thousand southern Taiwan students onto music and industry career paths. Since last year, Micro Movement has expanded into Chiayi with its own camps and courses to strong reception; this year it partners with 2026 Chiayi Rock Fest to launch the "Chiayi New Voice Audition" on 8/22, with Elephant Gym bassist Kai-Ting, drummer Chia-Chin, Tribe of Noise organizer Hsiao Ta-chien, SoundTrick Studio's Lu Yi-wei, and Jace Percussion Education's Chen Ying-chieh serving as judges. Winners get a shot at the 2026 Chiayi Rock Fest stage alongside Fire EX. and Shoalin, with the champion taking home a NT$80,000 cash prize.

![Kai-Hsiang Chang's brand Micro Movement runs a series of songwriting camps and courses in Chiayi](/images/news/2026-08-14-elephant-gym-chiayi-new-voice-audition/2026-08-14-elephant-gym-chiayi-new-voice-audition-workshop.jpg "Kai-Hsiang Chang's brand Micro Movement runs a series of songwriting camps and courses in Chiayi")

Chang also announced that Micro Movement will open a brand-new recording studio in Kaohsiung this September, with an opening festival on 9/5 featuring up-close performances from Elephant Gym, Shoalin, Hong Shen-hao, and Ofelia inside the studio itself. Chang shared on social media: "These past few years I've kept thinking about how to bring the recording experience Elephant Gym had in studios around the world back to Kaohsiung — how can southern musicians stop spending their album budgets on high-speed rail and hotel stays that have nothing to do with the music?" During the band's 2025 hiatus, alongside publishing his book "Setting Off on a World Tour!," he began preparing to open the Micro Movement studio in his hometown.

This year, Micro Movement will also run a series of professional workshops and lectures at the new studio from September through November, covering mixing and mastering practice, advanced production workshops, and behind-the-scenes crafts like recording, photography, and styling, with a heavyweight roster of instructors — including producer Thomas Chuang, who's worked with Waa Wei, Hebe Tien, and The Crane, and leading Japanese producer Ryo Konishi, who wrote the opening songs for the Japan Paralympics and the Osaka World Expo.

The Chiayi New Voice Audition kicks off 8/22, with this week's announcement of the eight selected bands: henÿ, Sit By The Water, Ying Xi ADM, Gamble Groove, Big Sword of a Brave Warrior, JĊANG, Paper People, and Zou Wenwen. For more on the audition, the Micro Movement studio opening, and workshop details, follow Micro Movement's official social channels.
