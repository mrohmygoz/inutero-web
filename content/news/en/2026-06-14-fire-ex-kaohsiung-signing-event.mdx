Fire EX. will hold their first-ever Taipei Arena shows on 7/18 and 7/19 — "ON FIRE DAY 2026 Taipei Arena Concert" — marking 26 years since the band formed. Both the first show and the added date sold out instantly, surprising the band. To thank their fans, Fire EX. returned home to Kaohsiung on 6/13 for their first-ever "fan-thanking signing event," dressing up as election candidates in campaign sashes to "thank voters" — a bit that had fans laughing at the band's knack for memes.

![Fire EX. members dressed as election candidates in campaign sashes to thank fans (illustrative photo)](/images/news/2026-06-14-fire-ex-kaohsiung-signing-event/2026-06-14-fire-ex-kaohsiung-signing-event-hero.jpg "Fire EX. members dressed as election candidates in campaign sashes to thank fans (illustrative photo)")

Kaohsiung had seen days of rain, and the crew had been preparing a rain contingency — but Fire EX.'s "sunshine-bringer" luck struck again, clearing the skies. Hundreds of fans lined up at the Kaohsiung Pop Music Center's Coral Lookout that afternoon for signing numbers, with the band saying it was their first time feeling like "real idols."

The event opened with Kaohsiung's own rising band BUTTER RIOT, performing "Punk Rock Paradise," "Empty Me," and "Your Power" — a band Fire EX. has mentored, whose album was produced by Fire EX. drummer Ko Guang.

Fire EX. members wore red sashes and arrived by yacht, docking to cheers and applause as "people of the sea." They launched straight into hits "Let's Go," "Stand Here," and "Nowhere to Go," instantly firing up the crowd. Frontman Yang Dazheng reflected: "We never imagined Arena tickets would sell this fast. The concert is in Taipei, but since we're from Kaohsiung, we wanted to come home and share this honor and gratitude with everyone in person."

With about a month to go before the Taipei Arena shows, Dazheng spoke emotionally before the closing song, "Night Bus": "Except for Ko Guang, all of us live in Kaohsiung now. Looking back on the past few years, moving back to Kaohsiung was absolutely the right decision, and we're happy Fire EX. gets to grow alongside this city."

Fire EX.'s first-ever Taipei Arena shows take place 7/18 and 7/19, bringing together 26 years of live experience. The band also previewed heading to the US to record a new album later this year. For more, follow Fire EX. on Facebook or Instagram (@fireex_official).
