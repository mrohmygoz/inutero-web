Organik Festival reveals its full 38-artist lineup, converging on the North Coast peninsula 4/24–4/26. Helena Hauff, Ma Sha, and Lena Willikens join the spring gathering, with workshops and special experiences designed to leave every dancer with something to remember.

The 13th Organik Festival's complete lineup is now official, adding a new wave of artists from home and abroad — Helena Hauff, Ma Sha, Mac Declos, Montonn Jira, and more — performing in relay April 24–26 at the North Coast's peninsula secret land, connecting generations and scenes of sound from day into night for a spring escape from the everyday.

![Organik Festival atmosphere](/images/news/organik-festival-2026-full-lineup/organik-festival-2026-full-lineup-hero.jpg "Organik Festival atmosphere")

Among the added names, Helena Hauff draws on the essence of acid house, EBM, and industrial to string together sonic details that seem, at first, incompatible. New York rising talent Ma Sha, who cut her teeth at the landmark underground party Kindergarten, twists rhythms into something both danceable and confrontational. Lena Willikens blends eerie synth slices with unsettling industrial pacing into a sweeping, uncanny sonic art piece.

![Helena Hauff](/images/news/organik-festival-2026-full-lineup/organik-festival-2026-full-lineup-helena-hauff.jpg "Helena Hauff")

Mac Declos, a veteran of top clubs across multiple European cities, brings high-energy, rhythmically locked supersonic sound that keeps the dancefloor swaying. Thailand's Montonn Jira — DJ, producer, and sound engineer, best known as the founder of Wonderfruit Festival — brings The Enfold Sessions to the Golden Arc, unfolding an hour-long live soundscape amid mountain breeze and sea view. Rounding out the bill are Asia-scene talents Ouissam, OCCA, and Maktoop, along with Taiwan's own active underground names Umbra, Pocari Sweaty, and L-CC, joining the international artists already announced in January — Alarico, Andy Martin, Diskonnected, and DJ NOBU — for a total of 38 acts riding a wave of electronic sound this spring.

Beyond the music, Organik Festival is also deepening the on-site experience this year, upgrading its glamping options in partnership with outdoor specialist brand Camp & Go. Workshops range from sensory interactive yoga using fruit in place of massage balls, to a Free Style movement class that frees up habitual body patterns, deep-stretch Pilates, a collective yarn-knit installation, and a five-years-running plein-air painting session — carving out, beyond the dancefloor, a path back to reconnecting with nature.

The food lineup includes Kaohsiung-based independent coffee roaster Akau Coffee and C&C Butcher's black-pig sub-brand Hy Hy Minced Meat (which also supplies fine-dining restaurants RAW and Akame), covering everything from full meals to light bites and drinks to refuel dancers between mountain and sea. On the experience side, Recovery Dragon Chiropractic, which has provided backstage recovery services for electronic music scenes in Bali and Japan, will be on site to work on bodies worn out from hours of dancing, alongside mineral DIY, hand-dyeing, and ear-candle neck-and-shoulder massage experiences, so every attendee leaves with something personal to remember.

Beyond the festival itself, the "Organik Takeover" tour launched at Tokyo's WOMB this past January, joined forces with Permit in Seoul this March, and will land in New York, Detroit, and Los Angeles this May, later extending to Singapore, Vietnam, Bangkok, London, Mongolia, and India — carrying Taiwan's underground sound out into the world. Early-bird tickets for Organik Festival have sold out; pre-sale tickets and on-site accommodation are now available. For the full lineup, workshop details, and directions, follow Organik Festival on Facebook and Instagram.
