Fire EX. rents out theaters to help "Champion Road" cross NT$100 million at the box office, while announcing their July 18 Taipei Arena debut in the same breath. Fire EX. wrote the film's theme song "Today's Me" — frontman Sam says "I cried three times just watching the rough cut." The band is putting its weight behind Taiwanese baseball, inviting fans to see "Champion Road" and witness Team Taiwan's never-give-up spirit.

Golden Melody-winning band Fire EX., who just announced their July 18 debut at Taipei Arena, rented out theaters on March 27 and 28 at Showtimes Taipei Dome and Kaohsiung Dream Mall for private screenings of "Champion Road" — keeping alive the emotion the WBC brought to fans by inviting both baseball fans and music fans to relive Team Taiwan's run to the championship at the 2024 World Baseball 12 tournament. Band members Sam, Pipi, and Kase all showed up in support, hoping to help push "Champion Road" past the NT$100 million mark.

![Fire EX. is set to take Taipei Arena on July 18 this year](/images/news/2026-03-28-fire-ex-champion-road-box-office/fire-ex-champion-road-box-office-hero.jpg "Fire EX. is set to take Taipei Arena on July 18 this year")

Fire EX. was invited to write "Today's Me" as the theme song for "Champion Road" — a track that moved fans in the film and went on to become a song fans sang together throughout the 2026 World Baseball Classic. Frontman Sam recalls the writing process: "Director Lung-nan (Lekal Sumi) reached out to invite us first, and I said yes immediately, because I'm a baseball fan! I cried three or four times just watching the rough cut. Walking home afterward, I decided I had to write the song from a first-person point of view, echoing each player's inner journey and carrying their voices through the song. Basically, everything I felt after watching 'Champion Road' went into 'Today's Me' — it's genuinely moving."

At a pre-screening talk in Taipei on 3/27, Fire EX. frontman Sam, bassist Pipi, and drummer Kase joined the audience for a conversation. Asked about the reason behind the private-screening events, Sam said with feeling that the recent Taiwan-Korea game at the WBC had been thrilling — Team Taiwan struggled in its first two games against Australia and Japan, but from the game against the Czech Republic through to the win over Korea, the team's resilience in the face of adversity was admirable and moving.

Sam said, "I love how baseball brings everyone on this island together. Watching the WBC made me think of 'Champion Road' — through this film, you can see the moving spirit passed down between generations, and understand just how hard the road to today's Taiwanese baseball has been. As the band that wrote the theme song, we naturally feel a special connection, and we wanted to give 'Champion Road' a push toward that hundred-million mark." He joked, "I've been involved with plenty of films, and none of them ever crossed a hundred million — so this is the closest I've ever gotten!"

Pipi added: "I already paid to see 'Champion Road' myself. Everyone says it'll make you cry, but I didn't cry — not because it isn't moving, but because I'm tough. Honestly, from the opening scenes, anyone who knows Taiwanese baseball history will feel it — looking back at games we've lost, winning that 12-team championship really was something. A new generation of players is on the field, and the coaching staff is the old Team Taiwan, so that sense of generational handoff really matters." Kase said, "I'm honestly more of a baseball newbie, but I watched that Taiwan-Korea WBC game and it was genuinely nerve-wracking! I hear 'Champion Road' has a lot of tearjerker moments too, so bring tissues."

Beyond backing Taiwanese baseball through music, Fire EX. has also kicked off its own "Road to the Arena." At a recent Megaport Festival performance, the band surprised fans with the announcement of their first-ever Taipei Arena concert this July 18. Fire EX. sincerely invites every fan to buy a ticket and witness this major milestone 26 years into the band's career. Ticketing details for the July 18 Taipei Arena show will be announced soon — follow Fire EX.'s official Facebook page or Instagram for more.
