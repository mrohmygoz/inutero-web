The new music festival "VOICES," curated by veteran producer Chen Chien-chi, musician Han Li-kang, and Underworld Music founder Oliver, arrives at Taipei's Dajia Riverside Park October 3–4! After the first wave — Leah Dou, Crowd Lu, puppiebelly, and more — set fans buzzing, the festival reveals its second lineup wave today (8/13): Golden Melody queen Aimee Chou, four-time Golden Melody Best Male Vocalist nominee Zhou De, rising trend-setter moon tang, plus "cyber-Taike" band Shoalin, psychedelic rock band Wendy Wander, Gen-Z singer-songwriter 163braces, and companion-style band Huan Huan.

![VOICES 2026, Second Wave Lineup](/images/news/2026-08-13-voices-2026-lineup-wave-2/2026-08-13-voices-2026-lineup-wave-2-hero.jpg "VOICES 2026, Second Wave Lineup")

Golden Melody queen Aimee Chou, known for her piercing, deeply sincere voice, has released six solo albums and repeatedly proven her vocal chops at major concerts. She shared: "I'm thrilled and honored to be invited to VOICES — there are so many artists on the lineup I admire myself, and I want to hear them sing live along with everyone else." She says hearing the crowd cheer gives her enormous encouragement on stage, and she'll bring a run of classics to electrify the crowd this time.

![Aimee Chou](/images/news/2026-08-13-voices-2026-lineup-wave-2/2026-08-13-voices-2026-lineup-wave-2-aimee-chou.jpg "Aimee Chou")

Two rising stars drawing close attention in Mandopop right now are equally compelling: Zhou De, a multiple Golden Melody Best Male Vocalist nominee, and trend-setter moon tang. Zhou De builds a distinctive, intense musical aesthetic through cinematic narrative tension and dramatic arrangements; moon tang has won over Hong Kong and Taiwan with her dreamy, adorable vocal style — of mixed Thai-Chinese heritage and fluent in English, Cantonese, and Mandarin, she gives listeners deeply felt moments through moving melodies and sincere lyrics.

This second wave brings Golden Melody-level vocals alongside some of contemporary Taiwan's rawest, most multifaceted live energy. Iconic "cyber-Taike" band Shoalin brings experimental instrumentation and infectious retro Taiwanese-rock swagger to whip up a mosh pit at Dajia Riverside Park; youth favorite Wendy Wander's dual vocalists bring genre-blending alt-rock and psychedelic sound, swaying the crowd with romantic melodies.

![Wendy Wander](/images/news/2026-08-13-voices-2026-lineup-wave-2/2026-08-13-voices-2026-lineup-wave-2-wendy-wander.jpg "Wendy Wander")

Gen-Z singer-songwriter 163braces, whose crisp acoustic performances have sparked late-night binge-watching across the internet, will strike listeners' hearts with her clear, piercing voice and adorable smile. Companion-style band Huan Huan, once nominated for Best New Artist at the Golden Indie Music Awards, brings gentle, poetic folk-rock, moving the whole crowd with their transparent, healing sound.

VOICES' early-bird two-day tickets are selling fast on KKTIX — limited quantities, while supplies last, so music fans are encouraged to grab tickets fast.
