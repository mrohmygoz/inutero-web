L8ching releases spring road-trip single "Blue Highway," documenting a trip taken on a whim — traveling the east coast alongside Hsu Pen Yi Yen, Weikai and Ricky, and Ke-Le Chen — and puts it plainly: "There's nothing better than just going." Finished the night before the tour and first performed live the very next day in Kaohsiung, the track weaves in field recordings of east-coast waves and insects and African Asalato percussion for a beach-rock texture. A full album is now brewing, inviting listeners to reclaim a sense of adventure in everyday life.

![L8ching "Blue Highway" single cover](/images/news/l8ching-blue-highway-single/l8ching-blue-highway-single-cover.jpg "L8ching Blue Highway single cover")

"The voice of the free spirit," L8ching, releases the new single "Blue Highway" today (March 13), documenting a spur-of-the-moment road trip around the island last spring — a portrait of musicians going wherever the road takes them along the east coast, and of a resilient friendship that sends its blessings across different goals rather than demanding they match. The track was recorded together with tour companions Hsu Pen Yi Yen and Ricky and percussionist Ke-Le Chen, woven through with field recordings of waves, insects, and children's laughter captured live in Bisiri Coast — so that every listen conjures the shimmer of a spring sea again.

"Blue Highway" is built on a beach-rock foundation: acoustic guitar lays down the openness of the road, Ricky's violin adds a flowing poetry to the soundscape, and the African Asalato percussion Ke-Le Chen brought in gives the whole track a light, foreign texture. The song was only finished the day before the tour set out, and the very next day, in Kaohsiung, L8ching and Hsu Pen Yi Yen performed it together on stage for the first time. The two-week tour ran through Kaohsiung, Taitung, Hualien, and Yilan before returning to Taipei. L8ching shares: "We were on the Donghe suspension bridge, looking at the Milky Way, playing guitar — no matter when I hear this song, it sends me straight back to that moment." L8ching cut field recordings taken along the way — waves, insects — into the outro, making the track feel more like a sonic travel journal.

![L8ching](/images/news/l8ching-blue-highway-single/l8ching-blue-highway-single-portrait.jpg "L8ching")

A lyric in the song — "wanting to stay clear-headed means some loneliness / wanting to hold something back means some pain" — captures an inner journey through growing up. L8ching explains the line is a sketch of the mindset of "someone drinking alone in the corner" — someone who, even in a lively gathering, chooses to hold back and not speak up easily, which looks like self-protection but actually creates distance from the moment. "This was writing about my past self, a kind of proud loneliness that assumed nobody would understand — but actually, once I said what I was thinking, my friends caught me completely." The tour taught him to really feel the importance of speaking up more and cherishing time together.

This "Suddenly, We Just Went" tour ran on a "low-cost, high-ease" ethos — everyone pulling multiple duties, handling their own sound, staging, and photography. L8ching recalls: "It was spring, everything felt renewed — Ke-Le and I had just finished a talk-and-sing tour, and Hsu Pen Yi Yen had just played Megaport. Neither of us wanted to stop, so we half-jokingly started planning this over coffee — and it turned out nobody was kidding." L8ching and Hsu Pen Yi Yen's Weikai are ten-year friends and bandmates in Shuiyuan; this tour deepened that bond again. Percussionist Ke-Le Chen was "recruited on the spot" from the audience during an L8ching show in Kaohsiung — he was jamming along on his own with an Asalato in the crowd, and L8ching simply invited him up on stage, and he's been a steady collaborator ever since. Friend Huang Xuan handled photography and staging for the whole trip and even designed and hand-lettered this single's cover, while their cat Kohaku rode the whole way propped against the windshield watching the scenery, the calmest member of the trip.

Asked whether he worries about losing that sense of adventure as he gets older, L8ching laughs: "Going wherever you want alone is freedom; going wherever you want together is something to remember — and no, AI didn't write that." That spirit needs friends who answer the call — staying true to it means remembering there will always be people around whom you feel like your own judgment dips a little, but your luck and sense of humor both go up — people who keep breaking the daily routine and helping you find surprise and rediscover yourself.

L8ching, "the voice of the free spirit" and a double Golden Indie/Golden Melody-recognized artist, was nominated for multiple international awards in 2024 for "Pulso," a collaboration with Filipino sibling duo Ysanygo from the *South Expedition* project, and continues to venture across cultural borders. In spring 2025, L8ching, Hsu Pen Yi Yen, and Ke-Le Chen ran the "Suddenly, We Just Went" round-island tour, traveling with music as a way of journeying and sparking different musical moments through a looser, more improvisational format. Heading into 2026, L8ching is now preparing a full new album, with "Blue Highway" standing as the truest sonic memento of that journey — continuing to bring listeners along and reclaim a spirit of adventure in daily life.
