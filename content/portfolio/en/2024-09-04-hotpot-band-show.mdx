A dinner show, taken literally: a full bill of live music served alongside a bubbling pot,
in a room built for eating rather than for listening.

In Utero's first-ever self-produced show, *In Utero Present Vol.1: Hotpot Band Show*, took
over the hot-pot chain Chan Chi's Ximen flagship — the first music event that room had ever
hosted. The lineup: Golden Melody Award winner Panai, Fire EX. frontman Sam Yang, Golden
Melody jury-prize act Elephant Gym, Huan Huan, Mong Tong, and newcomer Chao Lin.

![Elephant Gym and Huan Huan performing in a private room at Chan Chi's Ximen flagship](/images/portfolio/2024-09-04-hotpot-band-show/hotpot-band-show-venue.jpg "Live in a Chan Chi private dining room")

Three time slots, back to back. Elephant Gym and Huan Huan opened — bassist Kaiting's first
show back after a car accident in July. Between songs, the In Utero team snuck in a birthday
surprise for drummer Chiachin: the whole room sang along as he blew out the candles on stage.

![Drummer Chiachin blowing out birthday candles on stage as the crowd sings along](/images/portfolio/2024-09-04-hotpot-band-show/hotpot-band-show-birthday-surprise.jpg "Chiachin's on-stage birthday")

Mong Tong and newcomer Chao Lin took the afternoon slot, and the night closed with two Golden
Melody names: Sam Yang first, Panai last. Sam had left his acoustic guitar at home in
Kaohsiung, so he borrowed Panai's for the set — the two of them trading one guitar back and
forth all evening.

![Sam Yang performing acoustic, sharing Panai's guitar for the set](/images/portfolio/2024-09-04-hotpot-band-show/hotpot-band-show-yang-dazheng.jpg "Sam Yang's acoustic set")

> Honestly, we just wanted an excuse to get everyone together again. This time, instead of
> hotpot, we did it with a guitar.

The room, the audience said, sounded better than they expected from a restaurant — "who knew
a hotpot place could host a proper live show." A first event that set the tone for everything
In Utero has produced since.
