A free community touring programme built around a single question: what happens when a band
known for keeping people company brings a set of last-century classics into a senior day-care
centre and performs face to face with the residents?

In Utero and the band Huan Huan launched *In Utero Present Vol.3: Inner Voices of That Day*, a
free community tour running May 7–9, visiting the Zhongshan Senior Residence and Service
Centre in Taipei, the Huatan Angi Day Care Centre in Changhua, and the bookstore Ū Tsheh-tiàm
in Beidou, Changhua — bringing cross-generational gatherings to seniors and neighbours around
Mother's Day.

![Huan Huan's full-house group photo at the Zhongshan day-care centre, Taipei](/images/content/inner-voices-of-that-day-zhongshan-group.jpg "Full-house group photo, Zhongshan day-care centre, Taipei")

## What we were actually solving

The brief was not "book a tour". It was to find rooms where the music would land differently
than it does in a venue — and then to make the logistics of those rooms invisible to the band.
To make the songs easy to sing along to at any age, Huan Huan spent the run-up re-learning a
stack of classics spanning the mid-century to the early 2000s.

> It's been a long time since I listened this closely to songs from that generation... it gave
> me a glimpse of a Taiwan that had lived through something hard and something beautiful — the
> same era our grandparents worked through to get by.

![Huan Huan performing at the Huatan Angi Day Care Centre, Changhua](/images/content/inner-voices-of-that-day-huatan-performance.jpg "Live at the Huatan Angi Day Care Centre, Changhua")

The final stop, at the Beidou bookstore, was open to the whole neighbourhood — singer Coco
even duetted with her own father on stage, and an impromptu karaoke session broke out
afterward. Several residents stayed in their seats after the last note, thanking the band
through tears. Across all three stops, attendance held far past what the team expected —
audiences stayed seated the whole time, sometimes just listening, sometimes clapping along.

![Neighbours joining the open community show at the Beidou bookstore, Changhua](/images/content/inner-voices-of-that-day-beidou-audience.jpg "Open community show at the Beidou bookstore, Changhua")
