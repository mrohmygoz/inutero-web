A free touring programme built around a single question: what happens when a band that usually
plays late-night rooms performs at two in the afternoon, for an audience that has never heard
of them?

![Huan Huan performing to a full room](/images/content/sample-live.jpg "Full-house group photo, Zhongshan day-care centre, Taipei")

## What we were actually solving

The brief was not "book a tour". It was to find rooms where the music would land differently
than it does in a venue — and then to make the logistics of those rooms invisible to the band.

> Ipsum sit mattis nulla quam nulla. Gravida id gravida ac enim mauris id. Non pellentesque
> congue eget consectetur turpis.

Every stop needed its own answer for load-in, sightlines, and volume. None of them had a
technical rider on file.

- Six stops across Taipei, Taichung, and Chiayi
- Zero ticketed shows; every date free to attend
- One touring rig sized to fit through a standard doorway

The programme now runs annually. Details on [the services page](/en/services).
