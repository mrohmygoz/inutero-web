![Collapse (血肉果汁機) performing at SXSW to a packed house](/images/portfolio/2026-03-26-taiwan-beats-showcase-sxsw/taiwan-beats-sxsw-hero.jpg "Collapse packed the venue the moment they hit the stage, drawing heavy media coverage")

South by Southwest (SXSW) marked its 40th anniversary with a fully revamped 2026 edition, and
six Taiwanese acts — Hung Pei-yu, someshiit, Collapse, Sorry Youth, Prairie WWWW and Ao Yu Shan
— took the stage in succession at "Taiwan Beats Showcase" on the evening of March 13 local time.
Organized by Taiwan's Bureau of Audiovisual and Music Industry Development, the showcase
returned to Austin's Venue 6 with the same momentum as previous years, opening that afternoon
with a "Day Party" that connected partners across Japan, Malaysia and other Asian markets,
matching the six Taiwanese acts with industry professionals from the US, Canada, Czechia and
Brazil — complete with bubble tea, popcorn chicken, braised pork rice and spring rolls for a
full cultural experience.

![A packed crowd at Taiwan Beats Showcase, held at Austin's Venue 6](/images/portfolio/2026-03-26-taiwan-beats-showcase-sxsw/taiwan-beats-sxsw-venue.jpg "Taiwan Beats Showcase drew a packed house at Austin's Venue 6")

Ahead of the show, this year's lineup had already caught international media attention — Rolling
Stone and others sent photographers, with 360 Degree Sound naming Hung Pei-yu a festival
highlight; Sorry Youth and Ao Yu Shan each sat for interviews with Starry Constellation Magazine;
and someshiit and Collapse were both invited to play additional official SXSW showcases. Austin
radio station KTSW 89.9's music director, Ethan McConnell, singled out Collapse in SXSW's
official listening guide, praising the band for pushing metal to its extreme while still
embracing a range of genres and holding onto distinctly Taiwanese elements — and showed up in
person to watch them play.

That night, someshiit opened with an all-star backing band that quickly had the room warmed up.
Ao Yu Shan made their full-band US debut, performing songs from their latest album Hidden Album
and reworking their signature track "Richard" with English lyrics for the overseas crowd.
Collapse packed the venue the moment they took the stage, with the crowd breaking into circle
pits through the set; during "Birth + Tiger God" the band shouted from the stage, "We are from
Taiwan! Taiwan is a beautiful country worth loving — come visit!" The next day, Collapse landed
on SXSW's own social media as a headline act, hailed by the festival as "the band you will
absolutely not forget."

> We're so happy to play SXSW — it's probably the festival in the world that embraces the
> broadest mix of music, people and cultural exchange. Even where we're different, we all share
> the same love and passion for music.

Making her first US appearance, Hung Pei-yu performed the English-language track "Passenger
Side," carrying its romantic, intimate mood out into the Austin night. Sorry Youth, playing SXSW
for a second time, closed out the night at midnight — sharing those words above in English
before "Your Love for Taiwan Is Different," and finishing with "Undercurrent," dedicated to the
Taiwanese community working and living in Texas, bringing the 2026 Taiwan Beats Showcase to a
close.

![Sorry Youth performing on stage at SXSW](/images/portfolio/2026-03-26-taiwan-beats-showcase-sxsw/taiwan-beats-sxsw-sorry-youth.jpg "Sorry Youth closed out the night, dedicating 'Undercurrent' to Taiwanese living in Texas")

This year's SXSW held its music, film and tech tracks together for the first time. Beyond
facilitating on-the-ground networking during the event itself, "Taiwan Beats Showcase" began
its matchmaking work well before the trip, helping participating artists connect more smoothly
with the international industry and continuing to push Taiwanese music onto the world stage.
