![Railway Suicide Train Taiwan tour promo photo](/images/portfolio/2024-09-03-railway-suicide-train-yesterday-once-more/railway-suicide-train-hero.jpg "Railway Suicide Train's 2024 Taiwan tour, Yesterday Once More")

Chinese band Railway Suicide Train returned to Taiwan after several years away, launching their
*Yesterday Once More* Taiwan tour in September 2024 across Taipei, Taichung and Kaohsiung — only
the band's second official tour of the island. This time around, the band reworked their live
show with a full five-piece lineup, re-arranging older songs with fuller instrumentation and
giving a familiar setlist a different texture.

![Railway Suicide Train band promo photo](/images/portfolio/2024-09-03-railway-suicide-train-yesterday-once-more/railway-suicide-train-promo-01.jpg "Railway Suicide Train band promo photo")

In Utero handled PR and marketing, tour planning and event production for the run — booking
venues city by city, pulling together promotional materials, and running the shows on the
ground, stitching together a route that ran from the north to the south of the island. For many
Taiwanese fans, it was a long-overdue reunion: a band whose name translates to something bleak,
but whose sound never was, bringing its reworked five-piece arrangements to Taiwan's live
houses for a round of "yesterday, once more."

![Railway Suicide Train band promo photo](/images/portfolio/2024-09-03-railway-suicide-train-yesterday-once-more/railway-suicide-train-promo-03.jpg "Railway Suicide Train band promo photo")

Most of what this tour left behind lives in the room itself — there isn't much of an official
written record, just a handful of promo photos and, scattered across a few cities, whatever
each audience carried home from the night.
