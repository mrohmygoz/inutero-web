![Working photo from the Golden Indie Music Awards nominee ceremony](/images/portfolio/2022-11-05-golden-indie-music-awards/gima-hero.jpg "In Utero's own working photo from the GIMA ceremony floor")

Established in 2010, the Golden Indie Music Awards (GIMA) is one of Taiwan's major awards for
independent music, spanning genres from rock and folk to electronic, hip-hop, jazz, R&B and
alternative pop. In Utero's relationship with organizer Being Music spans several editions:
PR and marketing support beginning in 2018, community management, nominee hospitality since
2022, and in recent years, curatorial work on the Asia Music Awards.

![Backstage working photo at the GIMA ceremony](/images/portfolio/2022-11-05-golden-indie-music-awards/gima-backstage.jpg "Backstage, where the In Utero team manages nominee hospitality and runs the show flow")

This is a collaboration with no outward-facing press release — In Utero's role at GIMA runs
behind the scenes: making sure every nominee arrives on time and is exactly where they need to
be, so the spotlight at the ceremony itself stays on the artists and their work. This set of
photos documents the work that happens where the spotlight doesn't reach, the work that keeps
the ceremony running.

![Nominee hospitality working photo on the red carpet before the ceremony](/images/portfolio/2022-11-05-golden-indie-music-awards/gima-red-carpet.jpg "Red carpet staging, the final prep before doors open")

From PR and marketing to nominee hospitality and, more recently, curating the Asia Music
Awards, In Utero's work with GIMA continues — one ceremony at a time, helping Taiwan's
independent music scene reach a bigger stage.
