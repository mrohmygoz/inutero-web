![Performing artists and guests at the opening ceremony of World Music Festival @ Taiwan 2024](/images/portfolio/2024-10-11-world-music-festival-taiwan/world-music-festival-taiwan-opening-ceremony.jpg "The opening ceremony's launch ritual, with artists and officials from the Bureau of Audiovisual and Music Industry Development and Wind Music")

Now in its ninth edition, World Music Festival @ Taiwan 2024 ran October 11–13 across the outdoor
plaza, Live House D and Legacy TERA at Taipei Music Center, staging 28 performances and hosting
17 international curators and media from 15 countries including the Czech Republic, Poland and
the Netherlands — building a three-way meeting ground for artists, media and industry.

At the opening press conference, Japan's ten-piece Minyo Crusaders had the whole room on its
feet within minutes: "This is our first time in Taiwan — we hope everyone dances with us
tomorrow too!" Italy's Suonno D'Ajere brought a three-piece set steeped in Neapolitan folk
tradition, and Taiwu Ancient Ballads Singers — the first Taiwanese act to play WOMAD UK's main
stage — performed in full traditional Paiwan dress.

![Taiwu Ancient Ballads Singers performing in traditional Paiwan dress at the press conference](/images/portfolio/2024-10-11-world-music-festival-taiwan/world-music-festival-taiwan-taiwu.jpg "Taiwu Ancient Ballads Singers, the first Taiwanese act to play WOMAD UK's main stage")

Wang Shu-fang, Director-General of the Bureau of Audiovisual and Music Industry Development,
noted in her remarks that the festival has drawn 260,000 attendees to date. Wind Music's founder
Yang Chin-tsung added that this year's edition hosted 17 international curators and media and
drew entries from around 100 international acts for the showcase selection, creating a space for
genuine exchange between local and visiting industry professionals.

![Minyo Crusaders performing at the press conference](/images/portfolio/2024-10-11-world-music-festival-taiwan/world-music-festival-taiwan-minyo-crusaders.jpg "Japan's Minyo Crusaders had the audience on its feet from the first song")

Over the three days, acts from Malaysia, Canada, the Congo, the Netherlands and Buryatia shared
stages with Taiwanese artists including L8ching, Sheng Xiang & Band and Across the Lost Grassland.
The "Voices of Women" program paired South Korea's The Tune and Hohak with Buryatia's Namgar for
a cross-cultural set on women's narratives, while "Forging an Island" brought three-time Golden
Melody winner Luan Tan A Xiang together with Yijing Band to redefine what Taiwanese rock could
sound like. A "Global Market" food bazaar and a family drum-circle workshop rounded out the
festival for audiences of every age.

![A large crowd at an evening performance](/images/portfolio/2024-10-11-world-music-festival-taiwan/world-music-festival-taiwan-night-crowd.jpg "Each of the three nights drew large crowds to the evening performances")

This year's showcase selection drew a record 169 submissions from 35 countries. From a single
lively press conference to three days of performance, industry exchange and cultural programming,
World Music Festival @ Taiwan keeps narrowing the distance between Taiwan's music scene and the
world's.
